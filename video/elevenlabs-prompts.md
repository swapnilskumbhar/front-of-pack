# ElevenLabs Image & Video prompt pack

Generate the clips without dialogue or readable packaging text. Dialogue,
narration, UI and captions are added in ElevenCreative Studio.

## Shared continuity block

Append this block to every generated aisle shot:

> Contemporary premium supermarket in Pune, India, warm natural commercial
> lighting, realistic documentary advertising style, 16:9. Same two Indian
> women in every shot: shopper is 29, shoulder-length wavy black hair, mustard
> yellow casual shirt, blue jeans, canvas tote; friend is 31, tied-back black
> hair, teal overshirt over a white top, dark trousers. Natural skin texture,
> understated acting, physically realistic hands. Same aisle, basket and
> wardrobe. Packages have abstract graphics only, with no readable brand,
> nutrition, certification or regulatory text. No warped shelves, duplicate
> people, extra fingers, floating objects, logos, subtitles or on-screen text.

## Clip A — the claim wins (7 seconds)

> Smooth waist-height tracking shot following the shopper walking slowly down
> a packaged-food aisle. She stops, studies two colourful products and reaches
> toward the one with a large clean front graphic. Her expression shows mild
> uncertainty, not comedy. Camera moves from medium-wide to over-the-shoulder.
> Leave the phone out of frame. [shared continuity block]

Recommended first pass: Google Veo 3.1 Fast or Runway Gen-4.5, 1080p when
available, 16:9, audio off, one generation.

## Clip B — compare the fronts (7 seconds)

> Over-the-shoulder close shot. The shopper holds one product in each hand,
> looks only at their front designs, then begins placing one in her basket.
> Keep both packages generic and all small text illegible. Shallow depth of
> field, stable slow dolly, believable hands. [shared continuity block]

Use the final frame of Clip A as the start/reference frame when supported.

## Clip C — the intervention (8 seconds)

> shopper, smiles, gently pauses the purchase with an open-hand gesture, then
> points toward the shopper's real phone. The shopper becomes curious. No

> [shared continuity block]

## Clip D — informed choice (8 seconds)

> Match the same two-shot and aisle. The shopper now turns the second product
> around, deliberately examines its back panel, compares it with the first,

> The friend nods. The choice is thoughtful, not celebratory. [shared
> continuity block]

## Studio Agent instruction

> Build a 1 minute 55 second product-demo film titled Front of Pack. Begin with
> the supplied cinematic supermarket clips, then transition into the supplied
> real WhatsApp, website, cart, methodology and dashboard recordings. Preserve
> all real UI footage exactly; do not fabricate interface screens. Use the
> supplied narration, align cuts to sentence boundaries, add subtle grocery
