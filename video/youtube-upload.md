## Description

Most shopping decisions happen in seconds—and the front of a package is built
to win those seconds.

Front of Pack turns one product photo or cart screenshot into a concise,
evidence-backed shopper brief. It highlights whole-pack nutrition and %RDA,
allergens, ingredients, visible claim checks and relevant warnings, then keeps
the supporting calculation and source available for inspection.

Try it:

- Web: https://front-of-pack.front-of-pack-jobs-worker.workers.dev
- WhatsApp: https://wa.me/919325835971
- Source: https://github.com/swapnilskumbhar/front-of-pack

What the prototype demonstrates:

- One image with one or several packaged products
- Whole-pack values alongside absolute nutrition numbers
- Printed or clearly calculated %RDA
- Visible package-claim checks
- Exact-product web research when the uploaded image lacks the back panel
- English by default and twelve selectable response languages
- Website and live WhatsApp delivery
- Published decision logic and reproducible rating deductions
- Anonymous officer monitoring with latency and estimated cost per image

Built for the Build What Moves India hackathon.

Front of Pack is an independent educational prototype. It is not a government
service, laboratory analysis, medical assessment, safety certification or legal
compliance determination. Package facts take priority; online matches remain
identified and should be confirmed against the current pack.
